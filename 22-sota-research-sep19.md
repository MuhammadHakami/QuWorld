# 0. Headline

1. **Nothing scoops the composite.** No paper puts an input-conditioned analog Rydberg register with trained geometry inside a released industrial/foundation checkpoint under a pre-registered, parameter-matched, seed-replicated protocol. Four new neighbors must be cited and positioned (Section 2): the zero-init gated SE scaffold for VQCs in diffusion U-Nets (2607.09108, Jul 10), classical-hypernetwork-programmed circuit angles *and* observables (QFWP-ANO 2609.18655, Sep 16), Q-LoRA → H-LoRA (2603.02281: the phase-structured classical control matches the quantum LoRA), and the Hamiltonian-encoding QRC theory (2607.12035) that formally backs the analog-detuning design we use.
2. **Benchmark competitors moved on three rows.** Physics-IQ i2v single-sample top is now 45.5 (StrucPhysVideo-TI2V, Sep 17), TokaMark task 1-1 SoTA is TokaMind FT-Base 0.150 NRMSE (weights released) not the CNN 0.209, and Sophelio's cross-machine award requires S(DIII-D) ≥ 0.85 (we are at 0.7838). The Well's strongest public trunk is Walrus (1.3B, Polymathic), reported in *median* VRMSE (0.0831 TRL2D / 0.0059 RB / 0.0012 shear_flow one-step) — a different statistic from the official mean the ladder tracks. WorldModelBench and TORAX are unchanged; Pelzer's own group published a QCNN (2606.23411) that loses to their CNN.
3. **Adapter-design ideas worth GPU time (Section 3, ranked):** (i) trainable / input-conditioned non-local readout instead of fixed ⟨Z_i⟩; (ii) a zero-training register-configuration rule from effective-rank + scrambling-onset diagnostics (this *is* our "per-benchmark adaptation = configuration only" step, made principled); (iii) recurrent register state across rollout steps; (iv) a structured (quaternion / phase-structured) classical control arm; (v) a Pasqal-noise-model inference row via Pulser emu-sv; (vi) a DSE learnability number for the dequantization section.

# 2. Method landscape since Sep 6 (what is new, one line each, and what it means for us)

## 2A. Quantum modules attached to pretrained / frozen classical models
- **2607.09108 (Kim & Yoo, Jul 10) — VQCs in diffusion models via a squeeze-and-excitation scaffold.** Pool → project to n_q → core → per-channel gate `g = 1 + tanh(W↑ tanh(f(W↓ v)))`, **W↑ zero-initialised**; role-matched (144-param) and parameter-matched (16/32-param) MLP controls; 55 sampling seeds, SNR effect sizes; result: quantum ≈ matched classical (no parameter-efficiency advantage). Finds an **angle-aliasing failure** for unbounded inputs into rotation gates and fixes it with θ ← π·tanh(·). *Us:* same insertion pattern (our `Bottleneck`: pool → core → zero-init α gate) and the same bounding (`π·tanh(W h)` in `QuantumBlock`) — cite as the parallel design; our novelty is not the scaffold.
- **2603.02281 (Q-LoRA, Mar).** QNN inside the LoRA bottleneck of frozen CLIP beats LoRA by >5 pp few-shot; the authors then build **H-LoRA**, a classical Hilbert-transform (phase-structured) LoRA that matches it. *Us:* the "quantum-inspired structured classical control" is now the expected attribution arm (idea 4).
- **2608.22475 (Levy, Aug; already in survey)** residual PQC sidecar in ResNet-50: +0.19% mean, CI spans zero; **2601.21780 LEGO**; **2609.15394** (Sep 14) MobileNetV2 → 8-qubit VQC head for detection, circular vs linear CNOT topology; **2607.13466 PQFA** (frozen RoBERTa/ViT + parallel shallow VQCs, 2.2K vs 24K-param width-matched MLP control, quantum wins on MM-IMDb/N24News); **2607.21186** parameter-matched HQiCNN vs CNN on two medical datasets: "no architecture consistently dominates"; **2608.07822** (Aug 7) quaternion/SU(2) classifiers on frozen ResNet-18 features match real MLPs and beat shallow VQCs; entanglement gain reverses under pretrained-CNN features (−9.25 pp).
- **2608.06846, 2608.18155, 2607.04915** (already cited) remain the audit trio.

## 2B. Trainable and input-conditioned measurement (readout design)
- **ANO line (Chen/Tseng/Lin):** 2504.13414 (ANO), 2601.14433 (ICASSP 2026, super-resolution; code `hsinyilin19/ANO_SR`, PennyLane: k-local trainable Hermitian `H = A + A†` with separate LR for observable params), **2605.15410 diagonal ANO** (diagonal observables are canonical representatives modulo unitary similarity: same function space, O(2^k) instead of O(4^k) parameters), 2607.24399 (MTSF-ANO, ETT forecasting), **2609.18655 QFWP-ANO (Sep 16)**: a classical hypernetwork programs circuit angles and/or k-local observables per input (encoder MLP → linear decoder for θ, 2-layer MLP for observable params), lowest MSE in 16/20 ETT settings. *Us:* our readout is fixed ⟨Z_i⟩ from `probs @ zdiag`. Because we hold the full `probs`, a **diagonal-ANO readout is a linear map on `probs`** (any k-local diagonal observable is a marginal of it) — zero extra simulation cost. Input-conditioning the observable is a second hypernet path (we already condition detunings). Idea 1.

## 2C. Analog / Hamiltonian encoding and reservoir design theory
- **2607.12035 (Xia et al., Jul 13)** Pauli–Liouville treatment: with **inputs in the Hamiltonian generator** (our detuning encoding) the update map is a transcendental matrix function → infinite-order nonlinearity in the input; echo-state property comes from the Liouvillian gap; conditions: bounded drive relative to the gap, non-trivial drive term, `[L0, L1] ≠ 0` (Z-detunings vs X-drive: satisfied). Qubit-reset/state-encoding schemes get *all* nonlinearity from the classical encoding map. *Us:* cite as the theoretical backing for analog detuning encoding over angle encoding; our `π·tanh` bounding is the "bounded drive" condition.
- **2607.08037** Hamiltonian reservoir computing on an analog superconducting array and a digital circuit: comparable power, analog more hardware-efficient. **2602.00610 (Feb)** Rydberg-array QRC: single-step QRC (reset each input) is robust; multi-step (recurrent) QRC degrades under sampling noise and depends on the dynamical phase.
- **2608.25511 (Aug 26)** 2D Ising reservoirs (N ≤ 20): forecasting precision peaks at the **onset of scrambling** (OTOC saturation C̄ between frozen <1 and scrambled >2) and grows superextensively there; no fixed parameter works across N — track the onset. **2607.09445** scalable diagnostics: task-independent order-statistics expressivity score vs a Haar baseline + task-dependent **effective rank of the feature matrix** (cost independent of Hilbert dimension). **2603.21371** memory–nonlinearity trade-off unifies memory restriction, weak measurement, edge-of-chaos and dissipation. **2608.28440** QRC↔QELM are limits of one architecture, set by how many qubits are re-encoded per step. **2607.01187** symmetry-preserving readout (measure all shifted copies). **2609.09292** dual-unitary brickwork reservoirs mitigate finite-shot concentration. **2609.19308 (Sep 16)** 4,500-qubit D-Wave reverse-annealing reservoir: "a non-interacting reservoir retains no memory of its input" — convergent with our V_ij = 0 ablation. **2607.19506** LLM-guided QRC architecture search (encoding, depth, topology, measurement features, reset policy) beats random/evolutionary/TPE on 2 of 3 tasks — relevant to our register-configuration bake-off.

## 2D. Neutral-atom hardware realism
- **2607.28364 (Pasqal, Jul 30)** noise-aware emulation with **Pulser + emu-mps / emu-sv** (thermal motion & Doppler, position disorder, laser intensity/phase noise, SPAM η/ε/ε′, T1/T2 Lindblad, Ω/δ calibration offsets); validated on three Pasqal QPUs (FC1, SA1, Ruby): measured observables fall inside the emulator's envelopes. **2602.14641** Aquila QRC on a medical dataset: hardware features *more robust across splits* than noiseless emulation (regularising effect). **2510.04747** Aquila QRC (position and local-detuning encodings) on credit default: noise degrades. **2605.06727 (May)** cold-atom reservoir + guided autoencoder with **a differentiable surrogate of the quantum layer for end-to-end backprop; latents encoded as pulse detunings of a Rydberg Hamiltonian** — the closest training pipeline to ours (they train through a surrogate; we train through the exact statevector). **2605.21276** logical neutral-atom processor kernel solver. *Us:* Pulser's noise model is the concrete route to the "noisy inference" row reviewers asked for (idea 5).

## 2E. Quantum for PDE / CFD / plasma surrogates
- **2608.27626 Quantum SEDONet** (spectral Fourier/Chebyshev trunk features, −36…−54% L2 on four PDEs); **2608.23119** QRC + physics-informed corrector for reduced-order KS/Burgers; **2609.09268** spacetime VQA reconstruction of Burgers/KS from sparse sensors; **2606.31351** hybrid surrogate for the LBM collision operator (data re-uploading Fourier series, relates VQC expressibility/effective dimension to task error); **2606.18713** photonic trainable measurement for PINN fields ("phase-complexity transition": classical suffices in smooth regimes); **2609.19239** lazy-training/NTK theory for QPINNs; **2608.00850, 2608.03029, 2608.29894** QPINN variants (optimisation, not expressivity, is the bottleneck); **2604.04828 HQ-LP-FNO** (VQC mixer in FNO spectral blocks, parameter count independent of mode budget; −15.6% params, −26% error on laser-processing 3D); CV-QFNO (Entropy 28(7):737); **2507.08746 PHQFNO**; **2608.24150** hybrid tensor-network CFD; **2509.04006** 5-qubit Ising reservoir for truncated Navier–Stokes/Lorenz; **2307.03053 Pfeffer** RB reservoir (Section 1). Fusion/plasma: **no quantum entries** on TokaMark, Sophelio or GS-equilibrium benchmarks; classical SoTA = TokaMind (Section 1), FusionMAE (2509.12945), GyroSwin, SOLPS-NN, LIUQE-PINN (TCV, <100 µs).

## 2F. Attribution, dequantization, resources
- **2608.02325 (Wang, Du, Tao, Aug 3)** *dynamical stabilizer entropy* (DSE): how broadly an expectation-value function `f(x) = Σ_ω Φ_ω(x) Tr(ρ0 Q_ω)` of a tunable circuit spreads over its trigonometric modes Λ = {−1,0,1}^d; classical surrogates need polynomial samples iff DSE = O(log N). *Us:* a measurable quantity for the Phase-E "surrogates reproduce to 2e-5" result (idea 6). **2608.05240** "one qubit can beat one bit": the resource is *measurement incompatibility*, fixed-readout schemes are simulable. **2608.05595** late fusion of independently trained sub-circuits matches full reconstruction within 0.04 at exponentially lower cost, more robust to shot noise — direct support for our 3×10q `MultiRegister` (independent registers, classical head) over one 30q register. **2608.19497** hybrid vs structurally aligned classical forecaster: similar held-out error, different NTK geometry (less concentrated spectrum, smaller drift), fewer params (125 vs 281). **2608.01194** (Monbroussou et al.) and **2607.18552** are the current reviews of hybrid QNNs and QRC.

## 2G. World models
- **2608.19779 (Aug 20)** is the current arXiv id of Lumbreras–Ma–Thompson–Gu "irreducible quantum advantage in aligning world models with reality" (single-qutrit exact model vs every finite classical model; check the bib key `lumbreras2026irreducible` points to this id). **2608.05371 QSWM** (quantum-*inspired*, complex/density-matrix latents; ECA rules 30/90/110, L=32; wins one-step 73.6%, collapses to 50% at 10 steps; parameter-, dimension- and normalisation-matched controls). **2605.18031** quantum sidecar architectures: stateful protected register vs stateless reset-and-reprepare (density-matrix simulations only) — vocabulary for idea 3.

# 3. Ranked improvement ideas for the register / adapter (each with a runnable spec)

Budget rule for all: the matched MLP control absorbs any new classical path (hypernet, feature expansion) so the comparison stays parameter-matched; official metric single-shot; 3 seeds.

1. **Non-local / adaptive readout (highest expected value, lowest cost).** Today: `probs @ zdiag` → n features per register. Arms: (a) fixed Z + ZZ correlators (n + n(n−1)/2 = 55 features per 10q register, still a fixed linear map of `probs`); (b) **diagonal-ANO**: trainable `W_obs ∈ R^{2^n × m}` (low-rank or k-local-structured, e.g. sum of 2-local diagonals) — equivalent in function space to full ANO (2605.15410); (c) input-conditioned diagonal-ANO (QFWP-ANO style: `W_obs = W0 + U·g(h)`), with the hypernet params counted in the budget. Test bed: TRL2D arena (`vrmse@1`, `vrmse@8`) then one industrial row (TORAX, minutes per arm). Cost ≈ 1 GPU-h total on the arena. Risk: more readout parameters → the MLP control also grows; keep the total at the 30-qubit budget via `_count`.
2. **Zero-training register-configuration rule.** New script `paper/register_diag.py`: for a batch of trunk latents compute (i) effective rank R_eff of the readout feature matrix (2607.09445), (ii) the OTOC-type saturation C̄ of the register dynamics under the input distribution (2608.25511), (iii) a Haar-baseline expressivity score; sweep detuning scale (the π in `π·tanh`), evolution times τ_l and blockade geometry scale; pick the configuration maximising R_eff with C̄ in the onset window. This makes "per-benchmark adaptation = register configuration only" a stated, reproducible rule instead of the defaults. Cost: forward passes only (GPU minutes). Validate on one row (TRL2D) against the default configuration.
3. **Recurrent register state across rollout steps.** In autoregressive rollouts keep the register state and re-encode only k of n qubits per step (QRC↔QELM continuum, 2608.28440; "stateful register", 2605.18031). Matched control: a GRU-sized classical state of equal parameter count. Test: TRL2D arena `vrmse@8` and the 13:30 rollout; caveat from 2602.00610 (multi-step QRC fragile under sampling noise) does not apply to exact simulation but must be stated. Cost ≈ 2 GPU-h. Medium risk.
4. **Structured classical control arm.** Quaternion/SU(2) MLP (2608.07822) or Hilbert-transform phase-structured MLP (H-LoRA) at the same parameter budget, in addition to `mlp30` and the Gutzwiller product-state arm. Minutes per arm on TORAX / Pelzer U-Net; then TRL2D. This is the attribution control reviewers now expect.
5. **Noise-aware inference row.** Export a trained register configuration (positions, detuning map outputs, Ω, τ) to a Pulser `Sequence`, run inference with the Pasqal noise model in emu-sv (GPU backend) for one benchmark's test set, report the metric shift; inference-inertness predicts a small shift. Installs on aarch64 (pure Python + qutip). Cost: < 1 GPU-h. Addresses reviewer concern #3 without any training.
6. **DSE learnability number.** Compute the trigonometric-mode spread of our register's input→⟨Z⟩ map (d = layers × n detuning angles per register) on sampled latents; report the order-2 Rényi entropy of the mode distribution and compare to the log N threshold (2608.02325). Analysis only; strengthens the Phase-E dequantization section.
7. **Late-fusion framing.** Cite 2608.05595 for the `MultiRegister` design; no experiment.
8. **Angle-aliasing audit.** Verify no unbounded path reaches a rotation (all conditioned angles are `π·tanh`; geometry shifts bounded by `MAX_SHIFT`) — done by inspection today; state it in the instantiation paragraph with the 2607.09108 citation.
9. **Configuration search baselines.** When the bake-off runs, include TPE and the LLM-guided proposer of 2607.19506 as baselines to CMA-ES (already parked).
10. **Symmetry-aligned readout** (2607.01187) — not applicable to trained-geometry registers; note only.
11. **Benchmark actions (SoTA-first rule):** TokaMind trunk for TokaMark (B4b; register on `h_cls`; their 2-stage warm-start; 3×3 arms; requires the `tokamark` package + local zarr, torch ≥ 2.10 (we have 2.13), Python ≥ 3.11 (3.13)); **Walrus trunk for the Well rows post-ICLR** (1.3B; `eval_onegpu_example_walrus.sh` exists; register at the processor output before the decoder; report both mean and median VRMSE); Sophelio classical-side lift to S ≥ 0.85 (consistency term via a GS-residual loss; trunk-native scalar head instead of RidgeCV) shared by all arms.

## 4. Framing reference (user-supplied Sep 21 2026)

- **The Quantum Insider, "What is quantum simulation, how does it work?" (M. Ur Rehman, Sep 18 2026).** General explainer. Its analog definition ("specialised quantum systems whose physical behavior directly models the target system") and its three-stage encoding → evolution → measurement structure are the exact frame for our register (`Bottleneck`: input-conditioned detunings → Rydberg evolution → readout). Its listed challenges map one-to-one onto our design choices: *encoding complexity* ("inefficient mappings waste scarce qubits") → configurable geometry, FW-B configuration rule, signal routing; *classical competition* → matched-MLP control and the no-advantage stance; *verification difficulty* → the dequantization suite. Its closing gap ("research demonstrations rather than sources of commercially useful insight") is what the ten-benchmark ladder answers. **Distinction to state in the paper:** the article's quantum simulation targets quantum systems (chemistry; cytochrome P450 ≈ 4,900 logical qubits, 2035–2038 estimates); ours uses an analog quantum register as a co-processor for *classical* physics inside a world model (closer to analog simulation / quantum reservoir computing). Cite the primary sources it relies on (Goings et al. 2022 P450 resource estimate; IonQ–AstraZeneca–AWS–NVIDIA 2025) rather than the blog.
