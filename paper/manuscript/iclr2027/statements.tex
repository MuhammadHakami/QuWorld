\section*{AI use statement}
This work was carried out by the authors with an AI coding agent (Anthropic's
Claude Code) used as an executing research assistant throughout the project.
Under the authors' direction and standing instructions, the agent implemented
the quantum-layer and training code, ran and monitored the experiments,
computed the statistics, produced the figures, drafted and
revised the manuscript text, and performed literature searches. All hypotheses,
pre-registered decision rules, benchmark choices, and claims were set by the
authors, who reviewed the code, the numbers, and the text; every reported number
regenerates from the released scripts. Literature citations suggested by the
agent were verified against the publishers' or arXiv records before inclusion.
No generated data or fabricated results were used; the agent's role is
analogous to that of a research engineer following a written protocol.

\section*{Reproducibility statement}
All experiments run on a single workstation with roughly 120\,GiB of unified
memory and regenerate from one command per table (Section~\ref{sec:protocol};
\texttt{REPRODUCE.md} in the code release). Code, executed logs, and the
pre-registration record will be released on acceptance; for review,
pre-registration is therefore self-attested. The released repository contains
the quantum-layer implementation with its unit tests, the checkpoint-surgery
and temporal-difference fine-tuning code, the evaluation scripts for the official
and sliding-start rollout protocols, the pre-registration notes with their
timestamps and decision rules, all training logs and evaluation arrays behind
every table and figure, and the exact package lock file. The official CNextU-net
checkpoint and The Well data are public; download instructions are included.
Statistical procedures (paired Wilcoxon tests, Holm correction, trajectory
cluster bootstrap) are implemented in tested modules and their outputs are
committed alongside the arrays they were computed from.
